# Compliance notes: Pakistan (Punjab)

These are findings from public sources, **not legal or tax advice**. Each item needs
confirming with a tax adviser or the Punjab drug licensing authority before the pilot.

| Topic                                      | What was found                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | What the product does                                                                                                                                                                                           | Status                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Sales tax on medicines                     | Sources disagree: some say a reduced 1% rate for allopathic medicines (Eighth Schedule), others say 18%. Some supplies are exempt (Sixth Schedule).                                                                                | Tax class per product; prices are tax-inclusive; one "No tax" class is created at setup. Receipts will show the tax breakdown (M2).                                                                             | **Confirm rates**            |
| FBR POS integration                        | Required for Tier-1 retailers under the Sales Tax Act 1990 (e.g. part of a national/international chain, shop over 1,000 sq ft, high electricity bills). Pharmacies are included if they meet a test.                              | Not needed for a single independent store. Chains likely need it, so it is planned with multi-branch (Phase 2). Invoice numbers are per till (`T1-000123`) and the invoice model will keep room for FBR fields. | Confirm for each pilot store |
| Controlled drugs (Punjab Drugs Rules 2007) | Schedule B and D drugs are controlled. Each sale is recorded in a register at the time of supply (date, prescriber, patient, drug, manufacturer, quantity, batch, dispenser); the prescription carries the register serial number. | Products carry a controlled schedule; controlled products always require a prescription. Register entries and a printable register come in M4.                                                                  | **Confirm retention period** |
| Drug prices (DRAP)                         | Prices are printed on packs (MRP) and change over time, so old and new stock can carry different MRPs.                                                                                                                             | Each batch stores its MRP; adding stock warns when the sale price is above the batch MRP. Checkout (M2) will warn or block.                                                                                     | Confirm block vs warn        |
| Receipt details                            | Registered businesses print NTN/STRN on invoices.                                                                                                                                                                                  | Branch has NTN, STRN and drug licence fields; receipt settings in M5.                                                                                                                                           | Confirm wording              |
| Patient data                               | Not researched yet.                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Keep patient data minimal (name, prescriber, prescription photo only where required); encrypt cloud backups (M6).                                                                                               | To research                  |
